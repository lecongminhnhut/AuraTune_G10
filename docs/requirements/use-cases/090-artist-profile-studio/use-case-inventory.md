### Goal boundaries and actor decisions

- **UC-090-001:** One public-profile discovery goal includes identity, biography, featured songs, albums, related playlists and navigation. These sections form one profile experience; empty states, missing fields, privacy checks, not-found and retry are alternative/exception behavior rather than independent goals. Playback and destination-detail behavior remain owned by their existing features.
- **UC-090-002:** Follow and unfollow remain combined as management of one personal account–artist relationship, preserving the existing model ID. Duplicate requests, hidden-profile owner unfollow and failed/uncertain outcomes are variants of this goal.
- **UC-090-003:** Returning to a private collection of followed artists is independently useful without changing a relationship, so list viewing remains separate. Feature 092 FR-009 explicitly permits non-banned User, Artist and Admin accounts; the actor labels above use the model terms Listener, Artist and Administrator. No role gains cross-account access.
- **UC-090-004:** Inspecting the artist's own catalog and aggregate statistics is a complete read goal, separate from modifying tracks. Authentication and ownership checks constrain every Studio goal and are not separate use cases.
- **UC-090-005 / 006 / 008:** Uploading a new track, correcting an existing track's metadata and permanently deleting a track have distinct inputs, outcomes and confirmation needs, so remain separate. Cancel, invalid duration and other validation do not receive UC IDs.
- **UC-090-007:** Hide and unhide/republish remain two directions of one visibility goal under the existing ID. Republish changes Artist preference on the existing hidden track, not a new upload; public availability still requires no Admin restriction and no deletion (D05/D07). Play-count preservation and exclusion from public discovery are required effects (093 clarification and SC-003).
- **Upload supporting-actor source review:** Vision Section 4.1 explicitly places media storage outside AuraTune and shows its services saving uploads there; 093-FR-010 requires actual binary persistence/permanent playable URL. UC-090-005 therefore records External Cloud Media/Blob Storage Service as the logical supporting upload role. Provider/interface and equivalence to the shared model's CDN label remain open, not inferred from delivery/streaming. No shared actor ID/model or internal-component actor is added.
